### How to use

1. Log in to **Hospital** (e.g., H01).
2. Scroll to “Add New DNA Profile”.
3. Fill in the fields with the values from one of the tables above (use a different Sample ID for each, e.g., X001, X002, X003, X004).
4. Click **Add Profile**.
5. Go to **Police** → **Find Relationship** → Compare `A001` with the new ID you added.

You will see the predicted relationship and confidence exactly as described.
