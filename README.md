# A Centralized AI-Driven DNA Matching Platform for Family Reunification and Missing Person Identification in India
## The accompanying research proposes a centralized and explainable AI framework for STR-based kinship inference.

## Overview

A Centralized AI-Driven DNA Matching Platform for Family Reunification and Missing Person Identification in India is an academic research project that explores the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI), DNA relationship analysis, and centralized data management to support missing-person identification and family reunification.

The system aims to provide a common platform for organizing DNA-related records and supporting coordination between hospitals, police departments, and government authorities. It explores how AI-assisted relationship matching could help identify potential biological relationships for further investigation and professional review.

This project is a research prototype and is not a validated forensic system or a deployed nationwide service.

## Problem Statement

Missing-person cases and unidentified individuals can involve records distributed across multiple institutions. Fragmented information and manual coordination can make it difficult to identify potential biological relatives efficiently.

This project explores a centralized, AI-assisted platform to organize relevant records, support DNA relationship analysis, and provide role-based interfaces for different stakeholders.

## Objectives

- Develop a centralized web-based platform for DNA-related record management.
- Explore AI-assisted DNA relationship matching for family reunification and missing-person identification.
- Provide role-oriented interfaces for hospital, police, and government users.
- Organize relevant case information through a structured database.
- Explore how AI-assisted analysis can support the identification of potential biological relationships.
- Encourage responsible, explainable, and privacy-conscious use of DNA-related technology.

## Key Features

- **Role-Based Interfaces:** Separate workflows for hospital, police, and government users.
- **Hospital Module:** Interfaces for adding and checking records.
- **Police Module:** Interfaces for case management and relationship-related information.
- **Government Dashboard:** A dashboard for viewing relevant activity and workflow information.
- **DNA Data Processing:** A data and model layer for working with structured DNA-related features.
- **AI-Assisted Matching Research:** Exploration of relationship classification using development data.
- **Centralized Record Management:** A common application workflow for relevant records.

Feature availability depends on the current implementation. Proposed research capabilities should not be interpreted as fully implemented or validated functionality.

## System Workflow

1. Authorized users access the appropriate role-oriented interface.
2. Records are entered, checked, or reviewed using the available application workflows.
3. Structured information is managed through the application's data layer.
4. The model layer supports experimentation with DNA relationship-related predictions on development data.
5. Candidate results require qualified review and appropriate forensic confirmation.
6. Government-level dashboards demonstrate how relevant activity and workflow information may be presented.

The nationwide matching process is a proposed research architecture, not an operational national service.

## Dataset

The project uses a dataset for development and demonstration. The accompanying research describes a synthetic dataset of 1,000 profile pairs across ten STR loci. These artificial values are not from real individuals and are intended for experimental purposes.

The dataset will be hosted separately on Hugging Face rather than maintained as a dataset file in this GitHub repository.

### Dataset Considerations

- Use synthetic or appropriately authorized data only.
- Do not publish real DNA profiles or confidential personal records.
- Synthetic data should not be represented as representative of India's population.
- Results from demonstration data should not be presented as validated forensic performance.
- Ensure local database and dataset files are excluded from GitHub if they contain sensitive information.

The exact database setup, dependencies, and default accounts depend on the current implementation.

## Limitations

The accompanying research proposes a centralized and explainable AI framework for STR-based kinship inference.

Important limitations include:

- The proposed nationwide system is not a deployed service.
- The described synthetic dataset is limited and cannot establish real-world matching accuracy.
- The research does not establish validated performance on real forensic casework or representative Indian population DNA data.
- Model performance, calibration, scalability, and false-candidate rates require further evaluation.
- DNA relationship inference has scientific limitations, particularly for distant relationships and low-quality samples.
- A model prediction is not proof of biological relationship or identity.

The prototype is intended for academic demonstration and research. It must not be used to make real-world identity, family relationship, child-welfare, or criminal-investigation decisions.

## Privacy, Ethics, and Responsible Use

DNA data is highly sensitive and requires strong safeguards.

- Use synthetic data for public demonstrations.
- Never commit real DNA profiles, confidential case records, passwords, API keys, or private information.
- Apply appropriate access controls, encryption, audit logging, and retention policies in any future operational implementation.
- Ensure that any real-world use has an appropriate legal basis and institutional authorization.
- Treat model predictions as potential leads requiring qualified review, not final conclusions.
- Require appropriate forensic laboratory confirmation before drawing real-world conclusions.
- Assess applicable Indian laws and relevant forensic requirements before operational deployment.

This project is intended for educational and research purposes only.

## Future Scope

- Improve testing, documentation, and reproducibility.

- Compare machine-learning approaches with established statistical baselines.
- Evaluate precision, recall, calibration, and false-candidate rates.
- Investigate explainability methods for qualified human reviewers.

- Explore controlled pilot studies with appropriate authorization and independent oversight.
